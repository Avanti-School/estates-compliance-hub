COSHH self-check

Before using, storing, or working with a substance on site, work through this to check what controls apply under COSHH.
Based on HSE COSHH guidance (hse.gov.uk/coshh), current as of Aug 2026.
This tool gives a steer, not a substitute for your site's full COSHH assessment — always check the substance's Safety Data Sheet (SDS) before use.
If a substance is not clearly labelled or you don't have a data sheet for it, stop and ask before using it.
