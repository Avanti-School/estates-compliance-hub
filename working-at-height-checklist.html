Working at Height Checklist

Use this before any task involving ladders, towers, MEWPs, or roof access — by staff or contractors. Hierarchy first: avoid height where possible, then use collective protection before personal fall protection.
Planning contractor access for this job? Pair this with the Permit to Work tool so the job is formally authorised, not just checked.

Based on the Work at Height Regulations 2005 hierarchy of control: avoid → prevent falls (collective protection) → minimise distance/consequences (personal protection) → instruction/training.
This checklist supports but does not replace a task-specific risk assessment for higher-risk height work (e.g. roof access, fragile surfaces).
