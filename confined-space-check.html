Confined Space Entry Check

Run this before anyone enters a tank, duct, plant room void, or any space with limited entry/exit and a risk of dangerous atmosphere or engulfment.
Can this be done without entry? The first and best control is avoiding entry altogether — e.g. using remote tools, cameras, or extended equipment. Only proceed with the checks below if entry genuinely can't be avoided.
Based on the Confined Spaces Regulations 1997 and HSE guidance (INDG258).
If there's any doubt about atmosphere, access, or rescue arrangements — stop. Entry without a robust rescue plan is one of the leading causes of confined space fatalities, often among people trying to rescue a colleague.
