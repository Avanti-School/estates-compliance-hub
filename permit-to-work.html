Permit to Work

The gateway before a contractor or in-house team starts higher-risk work. Confirm the right pre-checks, then issue a signed-off permit record.
A Permit to Work formalises that risk-relevant checks were confirmed before work started — it doesn't replace the underlying risk assessment, method statement, or specialist checks (asbestos, working at height, etc.) themselves.
For licensed asbestos work, confined space entry, or any work you're unsure about — stop and get advice from your Head of Estates or H&S lead rather than issuing a permit.
